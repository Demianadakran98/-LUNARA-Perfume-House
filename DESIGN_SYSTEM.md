# Lunara — Design System

## Brand

Lunara is presented as an editorial fragrance house: quiet luxury, clear product discovery and a human WhatsApp checkout.

## Color roles

- Ivory page: `#F7F3EB`
- Surface: `#FFFDF8`
- Charcoal ink: `#1A1714`
- Muted text: `#746C63`
- Warm bronze: `#8A6947`
- Soft bronze: `#C8A980`
- Success: `#2F6A4D`
- Danger: `#9B443C`
- Border: `#DED6CA`

## Typography

- Display: Georgia / Times New Roman
- UI/body: Inter or system sans-serif
- Uppercase labels: 10–12 px with letter spacing

## Principles

1. Keep discovery clear before decoration.
2. Make price, availability and variants easy to scan.
3. Keep primary actions obvious and keyboard accessible.
4. Use whitespace as part of the premium visual language.
5. Keep checkout human and transparent: no automatic payment or delivery.
